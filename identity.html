PROJECT DESCRIPTION

In a world where twins are deemed a bad omen, two identical siblings are separated at birth and raised in secrecy. Unaware of each other's existence, players embody these twins, navigating their separate lives while uncovering clues about their shared identity. Their cold-blooded aristocratic parents, obsessed with creating a perfect successor, pit them against one another in a twisted competition to determine who is the "suitable" heir. As they confront moral dilemmas of trust and betrayal, the twins must decide whether to unite against their parents' sinister plans or let rivalry tear them apart.
