PROJECT DESCRIPTION

“I want to befriend them, they seem like a nice friend...”

Nothing will come after that if we just stop at looking at people from afar and make assumptions even though we want to make friends. The alleyways where Thu, Dan, and Duong meet in their same dream - taken inspiration from Vietnamese Hems where the crossroads and cables intertwine with each other crazily just like humans' connections!

01

Character Design

3 characters = 3 different personalities = 3 different starter colors.
Blue = Cold tone depicts Introversion
Red = Hot tone depicts Extroversion
Yellow = Neutral tone depicts Ambiversion
Greyscale = Shades & Hues = The characters’ surroundings that forms their tones.

Why 3 starter colors + greyscales?
My idea is that when 3 of them make an effort to connect and open up, they will “see more colors” in life as they mixed their personalities together. However if they avoid to do so, their life will be just monotonous ever.

Why pastel-y colors?
These vintage shades also come from Saigon’s old alleyways, which I want to bring in my story environment since I have been living here for quite some time to do my study.

02

Backgrounds

Real Inspiration from the pictures I took of alleyways in District 4 HCMC (Hem Doan Van Bo) - where there are a lot of significant and typical Hem’s traits.

I used real alleys as my inspiration because as I mentioned above, I thought it’d be nice to bring in some authenticity of my living city as well as putting the concept of connectivity through the abstract meaning of Hems - conections, lost, ... Additionally, by installing the iconic messy electric cables of us Vietnamese into the backgrounds and use them as passive guides along the story is also another way of me depicting connections in this project.

With the main color to be grey, the backgrounds create good contrast with the characters without being too distracting despite all the details.

03

CGs
