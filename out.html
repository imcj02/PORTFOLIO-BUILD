PROJECT DESCRIPTION

OUT - by Team Zero Zero Seven

A VR experience that places you inside a maze steeped in darkness, where only the guidance of a clown fish can help you to find your way OUT.

You are lost in the dark, then you find Nimo the little fish! He is a playful guy and he will lead you through the water. When you need direction, a specific hand gesture can help you find where Nimo has gone to. What will be waiting for you when you find him?

In this project, together with the use of spatial sounds, we brought in live hand gesture tracking as the control and attempted to implement the buoyancy of underwater movement to create an immersive experience.

Credits to my other teammates: Jiawen Hu, Xinyan Chen, Holly Lamprey
